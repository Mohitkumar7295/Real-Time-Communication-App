Workflow

How a meeting happens

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Log in with your email and verify your identity with a 6-digit one-time code sent directly to your inbox.

  2. 2

    Open a room

    Create a room with one click and copy the 6-character room code. Send it to up to 4 other colleagues or friends.

  3. 3

    Talk and collaborate

    Connect face to face over WebRTC, share your screen, sketch on the synchronized whiteboard, and pass documents.

Capabilities

Built for small, focused teams

Video calls

Crisp multi-party audio and video mesh for small groups of up to five participants, directly peer-to-peer.

Screen sharing

Present your entire desktop, a browser tab, or a single application window with seamless webcam fallback on stop.

File sharing

Share PDFs, images, archives, and design notes up to 10 MB directly with everyone in the active room.

Shared whiteboard

Sketch diagrams and write equations simultaneously. Late joiners automatically receive the full canvas history.

Group chat

In-room text messaging with timestamps and unread notification badges for questions and links.

Secure sign-in

BCrypt password hashing and mandatory two-step email verification codes (2FA) keep accounts safe from credential theft.

Security & Privacy

Clear about what we protect

MeetApp is built to be private, inspectable, and honest about its boundaries. We never sell your data or place third-party trackers on your calls.

  • Encrypted media streams Peer-to-peer video, audio, and screen sharing are protected by mandatory DTLS-SRTP encryption in the browser.
  • TLS transport security All REST requests and WebSocket signaling messages are transported over HTTPS and WSS connections.
  • Two-step login with Email OTP Every login requires a cryptographically generated 6-digit code. Plaintext passwords and codes are never logged or exposed.
  • In-transit chat and temporary file storage Chat messages and files are transmitted securely over TLS to our signaling backend for room distribution. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted.
